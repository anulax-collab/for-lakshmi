A LETTER FROM MY HEART

My Love,

Happy birthday, BB. ❤️

I honestly don't know where to begin, because there is so much I want to tell you. Sometimes I think about how randomly we met and how something that started with a simple conversation became such an important part of my life. If someone had told me back then that one day I'd be making a whole little birthday website for you, I probably wouldn't have believed them.

From our first conversations to those late nights when we kept talking until 4 AM, from 16 August to finally meeting you, every little chapter has its own place in my heart. I still think about our time together, our walks, our food, our random conversations and all those moments when we didn't really need anything special to have a good time.

I know distance isn't always easy. There are days when I wish we could just meet without planning everything in advance. But even with all the distance, you have become such a special part of my everyday life. I love sharing the smallest things with you, telling you random stories and finding reasons to annoy you. And yes, chaata pdega sometimes too. :')

Jaanu, on your birthday, I just want you to know how much I appreciate you. I hope this new year brings you happiness, peace, confidence and everything you've been wishing for. I hope you get countless reasons to smile and feel proud of yourself.

Thank you for being a part of my story. I hope we get to make many more memories, visit more places, take more pictures and have many more conversations that accidentally last until morning.

Happy birthday once again, my love. Keep being you. You deserve so many beautiful things.

Lots of love, always. ❤️
